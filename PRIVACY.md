# Privacy Policy for Text Scanner

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

## Data Collection and Use
Text Scanner uses your device's camera to scan business cards and documents. The images are sent to our secure servers (Alibaba Cloud) to extract text. No images are stored after processing.

## Permissions Used
- **Camera**: Required to scan documents.
- **Internet**: Required to send images to the server.
- **Storage**: Required to save exported CSV files.

## Third-Party Services
We use the Alibaba Cloud API for text extraction.
